      Pressure Analysis & Response Curves

      Mathematical definition of match pressure, high-leverage thresholds, over-by-over distribution, and player response timelines across Pressure Index bands.

      Pressure Index (PI) Formulation

      Pressure Index (PI)

      PI = 100 × Balance × [0.30 + 0.70 × Stage]

      Represents game tension on a continuous 0–100 scale. Scales match balance by stage progression with a 0.30 baseline floor.

      Match Balance

      Balance = 1 - 2 × |WP - 0.50|

      WP = Cross-fitted Win Probability. Balance = 1.0 in dead-even 50/50 match states; 0.0 in blowouts.

      Match Stage

      Stage = balls_elapsed / 240

      Fraction of the maximum 240 regulation deliveries completed in the match.

      Pressure Share by Over (Innings 1 vs Chase)

      Match Situation Difficulty & High-Pressure Cutoffs

      1st Innings Cutoff: PI ≥ 56.3

      Top 25% of first-innings deliveries. Peak pressure occurs during death overs (16–20) when setting competitive par totals.

      Chase (2nd Inn) Cutoff: PI ≥ 32.9

      Top 25% of chase deliveries. Lower threshold reflects the acute binary nature of chases where any tight balance creates intense leverage.

      Topic 7

      Methodology & Statistical Validation

      Mathematical formulations, sample-size shrinkage, context baselines, feature registry, and statistical limitations.

      Data Sources & Data Audit

      Analysis spans 883 matches audited down to 818 clean T20 matches with complete ball-by-ball data (357 IPL, 431 T20Is; 2022–2026). Rain-affected, single-innings, and super-over balls were excluded from probability fitting.

      Pressure Classification

      Every ball is assigned a Pressure Index: PI = 100 × Balance × (0.30 + 0.70 × Stage). Balls in the top 25% of PI for each innings (≥ 56.3 1st innings; ≥ 32.9 2nd innings) are designated high pressure.

      Official Scoring Formula (Approved Model)

      Official Player Score Formula

      Score = 50 + 15 × (0.40 × Z-output + 0.20 × Z-wickets + 0.20 × Z-lift + 0.20 × Z-consistency)

      Approved Weights: 40% Output · 20% Wickets · 20% Lift · 20% Consistency.

      • Z-output: Standardised output vs expected performance (RAE per 100 balls).
      • Z-wickets: Standardised wickets / dismissals vs expected (DAE per 100 balls).
      • Z-lift: Standardised improvement under pressure vs player's normal performance (Delta).
      • Z-consistency: Standardised consistency (% of pressure innings/spells above par).

      Sample-Size Shrinkage

      Shrinkage Factor = n / (n + 100)

      Empirical Bayes shrinkage applied to each raw metric before standardisation:

      • n: Player's total high-pressure deliveries.
      • k = 100: Prior sample constant. Shrinks low-sample performers toward league average (Z=0), preventing small-sample flukes from topping rankings.

      Standardisation (Z-Scores)

      Z = (Metric_shrunk - Mean) / SD

      Standardised across the 69 eligible players in the respective category (mean = 0, standard deviation = 1).

      Scaled by × 15 and centered at 50, creating a natural score distribution typically between 30 and 80.

      Eligibility Criteria

      Minimum 200 High-Pressure Balls

      Ensures sufficient statistical power and shrinkage factor ≥ 0.667. Eliminates small-sample outliers.

      Minimum 15 High-Pressure Matches

      Demands repeat appearances across separate match contexts and opposition bowling attacks.

      Minimum 6 Balls / Inning Filter

      For consistency calculations, single-ball cameos are excluded to prevent variance contamination.
